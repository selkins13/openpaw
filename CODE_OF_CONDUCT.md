# Code of Conduct

## Our commitment

We are committed to providing a welcoming, safe, and inclusive community for
everyone, regardless of background, identity, experience, or ability.

## Expected behavior

- Be respectful, constructive, and empathetic.
- Welcome questions and differing levels of experience.
- Give and accept technical feedback in good faith.
- Prioritize the safety and welfare of people and animals.
- Respect privacy and obtain consent before sharing personal information.

## Unacceptable behavior

Harassment, discrimination, threats, unwanted sexual attention, deliberate
intimidation, doxxing, and sustained disruption are not tolerated.

## Reporting and enforcement

Report conduct concerns privately to the repository maintainers. Do not open a
public issue containing sensitive personal information. Maintainers may remove
content, reject contributions, temporarily restrict participation, or
permanently ban participants when necessary to protect the community.

Reports will be handled as confidentially and promptly as practical. Anyone
involved in a report must recuse themselves from its resolution.
