# Prototype Bill of Materials

This preliminary BOM supports early one-tile and six-tile prototypes. Exact
manufacturer part numbers, quantities, alternates, costs, and suppliers must be
recorded before a reproducible build is released.

| Component | Initial quantity | Purpose |
| --- | ---: | --- |
| Raspberry Pi 5, 4 GB | 1 | Local hub |
| Raspberry Pi Pico, RP2040 | 6 | Tile controllers |
| WS2812B LED ring | 6 | Tile feedback |
| Copper tape | TBD | Pressure-switch electrodes |
| Velostat | TBD | Pressure-sensitive material |
| EVA foam | TBD | Tile body and pressure mechanism |
| Pogo pins | TBD | Tile interconnect |
| Neodymium magnets | TBD | Tile alignment and retention |
| JST-XH connectors | TBD | Internal wiring |
| 22 AWG wire | TBD | Power and signal wiring |
| MAX98357A amplifier | 1 | Hub audio output |
| 3 W speaker | 1 | Audio playback |

## BOM completion requirements

Before a prototype revision is reproducible, replace each `TBD` with a measured
quantity and add manufacturer, manufacturer part number, supplier, unit cost,
and approved alternates. Safety-critical substitutions require validation and
documentation.
